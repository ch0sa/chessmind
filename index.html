📖 ChessMind Quick Guide

⚡ 1-Click Instant Analysis

ChessMind runs a 100% offline, native WebAssembly Stockfish engine inside your browser. No ads, no lag.

  • Auto-Analysis: Move any piece on the board and Stockfish automatically evaluates the new position.
  • Green Arrow: Directly points to the engine's best move.
  • Eval Score: +0.8 means White has advantage. -0.8 means Black has advantage.

✋ Free Piece Placement (Place Any Piece Anywhere)

Want to set up custom puzzles or position pieces freely without chess turn rules?

  • Click ✋ Free Move: ON to drag any piece to any square without restrictions!
  • Piece Tray: Tap any piece in the tray below the board (e.g. ♘ or ♛), then tap any square to drop it.
  • Delete Pieces: Tap the 🗑️ trash icon, then tap a piece on the board to remove it.
  • 1-Click Reset & Clear: Tap Clear Board to wipe all pieces, or Starting Position to reset standard 32 pieces instantly.

🎙️ Voice Commands & Press-to-Hold

Press and hold the 🎙️ Hold to Speak button, speak your move, and release!

SayAction
"White Knight to e5"Places/moves white knight to e5
"Black Queen to f6"Places/moves black queen to f6
"c2 to c5"Moves piece from c2 to c5
"Knight to e7" / "e4"Standard move
"Remove e4"Clears piece from square
"Analyze" / "Flip"Engine & board controls

Random speech or background noise is safely ignored.

⌨️ Text Input Box

You can also type any of the above commands into the input box and click Move or press Enter.
